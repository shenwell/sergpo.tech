*Here goes nothing.*

I've heard about someone who launched 12 products in 12 months. I've heard about Chinese vibe-coders firing off prompts like there's no tomorrow. But I haven't heard of anyone who could actually build a hundred finished products. And yet all the resources to pull it off are already here. The only thing missing is a community crazy enough to back the idea. Since it doesn't exist, I'll build it.

**[Product Lab 100](https://productlaba.ru)** is exactly that project. I'm challenging myself and committing to creating a hundred products. Start date: today. How will I do it? Honestly — no idea.

I came up with an idea recently. Not sure you'll buy it, but it hit me hard:

> A strong product isn't born from the desire to build something. It's born from the tension between a person and the problem.

Maybe not even a person. Which means you need to set yourself a task. Before, I was aiming for one product a month. Now the goal is much bolder: a hundred products — ideally faster, maybe one a week or even one a day. That automatically pushes me toward:

1. using new tools;
2. developing my own ways of building;
3. reshaping how I think;
4. going through every stage of acceptance.

So this isn't just mine — I'm inviting you to join. And start building products too. Right now, with no warm-up posts or extra announcements. On your own, or alongside me.

I don't know what this whole thing will turn into. Who will I become if I finish the marathon? But one thing I'm sure of: a major tectonic shift in how I see and build products will happen over the next three months. Short updates will go on my [channel](https://t.me/supervisionpw). If you, like me, feel change brewing and have a rough idea — or no idea at all — of what you'd want to create, come into the private community. I'll share everything I do in detail, and we'll cross-pollinate.

By the way, I don't know if I'll pull this challenge off. From my last two products: the first took 5 days, the second 4 days to a production-ready solution. And that was with force majeure, personal stuff, a day job running in parallel, long test runs, production going down, and arguments with the model. So there really are no excuses.

## Already in the marathon

To not just talk: whether anyone joins or not — I'm already in. Product Lab had a quiet launch very recently. I've already shipped the first product. Yes, it's about the lab itself — but I needed to understand what I was building. I think I've got it now.)

I wanted to say I won't teach anyone anything — and that's true. But explaining, showing, and pointing the way is easy. Plus you'll do the same as Pro group members, if you decide to join.
